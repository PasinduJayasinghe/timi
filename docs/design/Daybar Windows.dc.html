Daybar — Windows

Expanded window, wide mode and the collapsed mini bar. 1a is live: tick tasks, start/pause timers (one at a time — the previous one pauses and saves with a toast), drag rows to reorder, filter by type and collapse the timeline. Extra type choices: Meeting = amber, groups icon (timers you start for meetings) and Other = slate, category icon. Calendar events stay rose with a video icon. Also: Sheets & popovers · Calendar & Log · Alerts, settings, tray · Tokens
