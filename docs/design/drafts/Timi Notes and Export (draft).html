    Notes stay on this PC. Should they sync to Supabase like sessions, so they show on another PC?
  2. 2
    Make a task
    After Make a task, keep the note (as drawn) or remove it, with Undo?
  3. 3
    Note format
    Plain text only (as drawn), or also checklists and links you can click?
  4. 4
    Notes tab
    Today · Notes · Calendar · Log, and with the planner Today · Plan · Notes · Calendar · Log (8a, right). Do five tabs fit well enough, or should Notes be an icon next to Settings?
  5. 5
    Meetings
    Timed meetings are already sessions. Should Google Calendar events you didn’t time also go in the export, on a third sheet?
  6. 6
    Day boundary
    The export splits days at 6 am like the Log, so a whole night shift keeps one date. Is that right for your timesheet?
  7. 7
    Task rows
    A task worked on over several days gets one row per day (as drawn: #4821 on Mon and Tue). Or one row per task for the whole range?
  8. 8
    Time format
    Durations as h:mm (2:08), which Excel can add up, or as decimal hours (2.13) for billing?
