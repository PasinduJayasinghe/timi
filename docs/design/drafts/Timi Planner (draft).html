?Open choices for you
  1. 1
    End shift button
    A strip above quick add on Today (7i), and in the wide title bar. Should End shift also be in the tray menu?
  2. 2
    Forgotten shift
    A shift left open ends itself after 6 am once nothing has been timed for 4 hours (7l). Is 4 hours right, or should it just stay open until you end it?
  3. 3
    Same-day restart
    After End shift, Start shift begins a new day. “Reopen Friday’s shift” continues the one you ended (7k). Do you need Reopen, e.g. for split shifts?
  4. 4
    Goals
    Goals were step 3 of Plan my day. They now wait for the Plan tab phase. OK to leave them out of the first version?
  5. 5
    Reminders
    Should a planned block also remind you, e.g. 5 minutes before it starts (like task reminders)?
  6. 6
    Moved tags
    When you move a task to a later day, should it show a “moved from Fri” tag, like “from yesterday”?
  7. 7
    Hours
    Is a day’s available time your working hours (7 pm – 4 am, 9h) minus meetings? Should Settings let you change them?
  8. 8
    Week strip
    Week strip numbers: “5·6h” = tasks and estimated hours. Is that useful, or just the task count?
  9. 9
    Running late
    On Today, should a planned task that hasn’t started by its time show “not started” (7c), or stay quiet?
  10. 10
    Sync
    Planner data (goals, notes, blocks) stays on this PC only. Should it sync like sessions?
